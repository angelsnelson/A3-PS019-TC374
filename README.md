## 📋 Problem Statement Background

Modern data protection frameworks—such as India's **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023** and global regulations like **GDPR**—require organizations to identify all stored Personally Identifiable Information (PII) and map it directly to declared business purposes and retention schedules.

In real-world IT environments, personal data frequently accumulates across unindexed SQL database tables and flat CSV exports without clear documentation or audit trails. 

### 🎯 Key Challenges Addressed
1. **Unindexed Data Sprawl:** PII fields hidden across databases without centralized metadata tracking.
2. **Metadata Gap Risk:** Personal data retained beyond compliance limits or used without documented purpose.
3. **Over-Automation Risks:** Avoiding automated, legally ambiguous compliance decisions by strictly generating objective **technical evidence** for human Data Protection Officer (DPO) review.

---

## 💡 Solution & Technical Architecture

Our engine is an automated **Privacy Evidence Inventory Tool** designed to parse SQL databases and CSV spreadsheets, detect candidate PII fields across **5 predefined classes**, and bind them against organizational purpose and retention registries.
